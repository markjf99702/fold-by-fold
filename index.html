Watch each fold happen on a real sheet of paper. Replay it, slow it down, scrub it back and forth, and drag to see it from any side.

Paper

    Reading a diagram

    Printed origami instructions use a handful of marks. Every step here shows the same marks on the paper just before it folds.

    Valley fold
    A dashed line. Fold the paper toward you, so the crease sinks like a valley.
    Mountain fold
    A line of dashes and dots. Fold the paper away from you, behind, so the crease stands up like a ridge.
    Fold and unfold
    Make the fold, press it flat, and open it again. The crease guides a later step.
    Inside reverse fold
    A mountain line across a narrow flap. Open the flap a little and push its tip up inside, between its own layers, so the tip turns inside out along the line.
    Turn over
    Flip the whole model over, left to right.
